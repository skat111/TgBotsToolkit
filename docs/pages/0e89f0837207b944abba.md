# Telegram Widgets

Source: https://core.telegram.org/widgets

Telegram widgets offer a quick way to add a [**sharing button**](https://core.telegram.org/widgets/share) to your pages, [**embed posts**](https://core.telegram.org/widgets/posts) from public [channels](https://telegram.org/tour/channels) or [groups](https://telegram.org/tour/groups). Widgets can also help your users [**log in with Telegram**](https://core.telegram.org/widgets/login) or view [**Telegram Discussions**](https://core.telegram.org/widgets/discussion) on your website.
